Puts the food you're cooking onto the range or fire while it cooks!

## Features

- Raw food sits on the range or fire, then turns cooked or burnt. Hopefully it doesn't all go up in smoke...
- Hovering a food in the cooking menu previews it on the range.
- Works on ranges, fires, TzHaar sulphur vents and the POH steel and fancy ranges.
- Supports both fish and meats.
